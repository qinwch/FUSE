# Artifact Registry

Large datasets, trained weights, generated posterior samples, and long-run reference chains are kept outside Git. The intended archival target is Zenodo. After upload, fill in the `Zenodo URL` column and replace `not-computed-file-exceeds-*` entries with checksums computed on the uploaded files.

Artifact placement convention after download:

- SBIBM generated datasets: `artifacts/sbibm/<task>/<budget>/`
- SBIBM trained models and posterior samples: `artifacts/sbibm/<method>/<task>/<budget>/`
- Gravitational-wave datasets and checkpoints: `artifacts/gravitational-waves/`
- Beta Pictoris b datasets, checkpoints, posterior samples, and PTMCMC reference chains: `artifacts/orbit/`
- Paper figures/tables generated from saved outputs: `artifacts/paper-results/`
